## Summary

Direct-Force Immersed Boundary Method (DF-IBM) computes forces directly from flow variables, avoiding user-specified penalty parameters. It excels in fluid-structure interaction but suffers from boundary slip error. Current research focuses on simple slip corrections, parameter optimization, and developing implicit DF-IBM to enhance stability and accuracy for complex simulations.
