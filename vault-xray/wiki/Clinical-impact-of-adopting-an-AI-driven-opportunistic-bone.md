## Summary

An AI tool (Rho) opportunistically screened routine X-rays for low bone density. Radiologists reported findings in 41% of flagged cases. Rho-generated DXA referrals nearly doubled, yielding higher true low BMD rates (87% vs 69%) than standard care, especially in older men and first-time assessments.
