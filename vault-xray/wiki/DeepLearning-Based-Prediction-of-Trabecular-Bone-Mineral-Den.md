## Summary

A deep learning model using lumbar CT accurately predicted trabecular bone density (r=0.934 with DEXA) and classified osteoporosis/osteopenia. In patients with spinal hyperostosis, CT-predicted BMD significantly outperformed DEXA for fracture-risk prediction (AUC 0.901 vs 0.464).
