## Summary

Researchers developed OsPenScreen, a deep learning model that detects low bone mass from routine chest X-rays. Trained on 77,812 paired DXA-CXR datasets using knowledge distillation, it achieved an AUC of 0.95 in external validation, offering a cost-effective, scalable tool for early opportunistic screening to prevent osteoporosis.
